Instano Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

The short version

Instano collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no servers.

What Instano is

Instano is a browser for Instagram's website that hides distracting elements (Reels, the Explore grid, suggested and sponsored posts) on your device. You log in to Instagram through Instagram's own pages, exactly as you would in Safari.

Data we collect

None. Instano has no backend, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporting. Your Instagram credentials go directly to Instagram and are never visible to Instano. Login cookies are stored on your device by iOS's web engine and never leave it, other than being sent to Instagram like any browser does.

Third parties

When you use Instano you are browsing instagram.com, so Instagram's own privacy policy applies to everything you do there. Instano adds no third-party services of its own.

Data deletion

Delete the app; all local data (cookies, cache) goes with it.
